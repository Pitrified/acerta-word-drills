# Privacy Policy - acerta

**Last updated:** 2026-11-14

## Overview

acerta is a vocabulary drill app.
It makes no network connection: the app asks Android for no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off the phone.

## What acerta collects

Nothing.
There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no account.

## Local data

Your drill progress is kept on the device only.
It is not sent anywhere and no copy exists outside the phone.

## Data deletion

All local data is deleted by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Children's privacy

acerta collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Source

The source code is public at <https://github.com/Pitrified/acerta-word-drills>, so what this policy describes can be checked in the code.

## Changes

This policy may be updated.
Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
